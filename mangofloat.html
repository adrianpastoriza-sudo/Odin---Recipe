Filipino Mango Float Recipe

A creamy, sweet, and refreshing Filipino dessert!

Finished Dish

Description

Mango Float is a popular Filipino no-bake dessert made with layers of fresh ripe mangoes, graham crackers, all-purpose cream, and condensed milk. It is known for its sweet, creamy, and refreshing taste.

This delicious dessert is easy to prepare because it does not require baking. It is commonly served during birthdays, celebrations, and family gatherings. It is best enjoyed cold after being refrigerated for several hours.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Chilling Time Servings Difficulty
20 minutes 5-8 hours 6-8 people Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe mangoes
  • 2 boxes (250 ml each) all-purpose cream
  • 1 can (300 ml) condensed milk
  • 1 pack graham crackers
  • 1/2 cup fresh milk or evaporated milk
  • Crushed graham crackers for toppings (optional)

Steps

  1. Chill the all-purpose cream in the refrigerator before preparing the dessert.
  2. Peel the mangoes and slice them into thin pieces. Set aside.
  3. In a mixing bowl, combine the chilled all-purpose cream and condensed milk.
  4. Mix the ingredients until smooth and creamy.
  5. Prepare a rectangular container or glass dish.
  6. Arrange a layer of graham crackers at the bottom of the container.
  7. Spread a layer of the cream mixture over the graham crackers.
  8. Add sliced mangoes on top of the cream mixture.
  9. Repeat the layers of graham crackers, cream mixture, and mangoes until the ingredients are used up.
  10. Add crushed graham crackers or mango slices on top for decoration.
  11. Cover the container and refrigerate for at least 5 hours or until firm.
  12. Slice, serve chilled, and enjoy your homemade Filipino Mango Float!
