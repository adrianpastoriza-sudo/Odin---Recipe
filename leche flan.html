Filipino Leche Flan Recipe

A smooth, creamy, and delicious Filipino dessert!

Finished Dish

Description

Leche Flan is a popular Filipino dessert made from egg yolks, condensed milk, evaporated milk, and caramelized sugar. It has a smooth, creamy texture and a sweet caramel topping.

This delicious dessert is commonly served during birthdays, Christmas celebrations, fiestas, and family gatherings. It is best enjoyed chilled.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings Difficulty
20 minutes 40 minutes 6-8 people Easy

Ingredients

For the Caramel:

  • 3 tablespoons white sugar

For the Custard:

  • 12 egg yolks
  • 1 can (300 ml) condensed milk
  • 1 can (370 ml) evaporated milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Separate the egg yolks from the egg whites and place the yolks in a mixing bowl.
  2. Add the condensed milk, evaporated milk, vanilla extract, and salt.
  3. Gently mix all the ingredients until well combined.
  4. Strain the mixture to remove lumps and make the custard smooth.
  5. Place the sugar in a leche flan mold or llanera.
  6. Heat the sugar carefully until it melts and turns golden brown.
  7. Spread the caramel evenly across the bottom of the mold.
  8. Slowly pour the custard mixture into the caramelized mold.
  9. Cover the mold with aluminum foil.
  10. Steam for approximately 30-40 minutes or until the custard is set.
  11. Remove from the steamer and allow the leche flan to cool.
  12. Refrigerate for at least 3 hours.
  13. Run a knife around the edges and carefully invert onto a serving plate.
  14. Serve chilled and enjoy your homemade Filipino Leche Flan!
