Filipino Spaghetti Recipe

A delicious Filipino-style sweet spaghetti!

Description

Filipino Spaghetti is a popular Filipino pasta dish known for its sweet-style sauce, ground meat, hotdogs, and melted cheese. It is commonly served during birthdays, parties, and family gatherings.

This delicious recipe is easy to prepare and has a sweet and savory flavor. It is best served hot with grated cheese on top.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings Difficulty
15 minutes 30 minutes 4-5 people Easy

Ingredients

  • 500 grams spaghetti noodles
  • 500 grams ground pork or beef
  • 5 pieces hotdogs, sliced
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups Filipino-style spaghetti sauce
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon sugar (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated cheese for topping

Steps

  1. Boil water in a large pot and cook the spaghetti noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Drain the cooked noodles and set them aside.
  3. Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat.
  4. Add garlic and onion, then sauté until fragrant.
  5. Add ground pork or beef and cook until browned.
  6. Add sliced hotdogs and cook for a few minutes.
  7. Pour in the spaghetti sauce, tomato sauce, and water.
  8. Add sugar, salt, and pepper. Stir and simmer for 15-20 minutes.
  9. Place the cooked spaghetti noodles on a plate.
  10. Pour the sauce over the noodles and add grated cheese.
  11. Serve hot and enjoy your Filipino Spaghetti!
